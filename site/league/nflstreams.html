All 32 NFL Teams: A Complete Guide for the 2025 Season

The 2025 NFL season is packed with excitement as all 32 teams gear up to chase the Lombardi Trophy. Whether you're a hardcore fan or just getting into football, this guide breaks down each team by conference and division—so you know who’s who this season.

🏈 AFC (American Football Conference)

AFC East

  • Buffalo Bills – Led by QB Josh Allen, still playoff contenders with a strong defense.
  • Miami Dolphins – Speed-driven offense powered by Tua Tagovailoa and Tyreek Hill.
  • New England Patriots – Rebuilding under new leadership after the post-Belichick era.
  • New York Jets – Aaron Rodgers returns healthy, hoping to make a deep playoff push.

AFC North

  • Baltimore Ravens – Lamar Jackson leads a dynamic offense; Super Bowl hopefuls.
  • Cincinnati Bengals – Joe Burrow and Ja'Marr Chase headline one of the NFL’s top duos.
  • Cleveland Browns – Deshaun Watson seeks consistency; dominant defense led by Myles Garrett.
  • Pittsburgh Steelers – Young QB Kenny Pickett shows promise; defense remains elite.

AFC South

  • Houston Texans – C.J. Stroud and rising stars look to build on a breakout 2024.
  • Indianapolis Colts – Anthony Richardson returns with dual-threat explosiveness.
  • Jacksonville Jaguars – Trevor Lawrence looks to regain 2022 form under Doug Pederson.
  • Tennessee Titans – A new era begins as the team rebuilds around Will Levis.

AFC West

  • Denver Broncos – Sean Payton’s squad trying to regain footing with a revamped offense.
  • Kansas City Chiefs – Reigning champions with Patrick Mahomes & Andy Reid at the helm.
  • Las Vegas Raiders – Defense-first team with Maxx Crosby; QB battle continues.
  • Los Angeles Chargers – Justin Herbert returns, aiming for a healthier and deeper season.

🏆 NFC (National Football Conference)

NFC East

  • Dallas Cowboys – A top NFC contender with Dak Prescott, Micah Parsons & a loaded roster.
  • New York Giants – Daniel Jones faces pressure to deliver under new offensive scheme.
  • Philadelphia Eagles – Jalen Hurts and a deep roster eye a return to the Super Bowl.
  • Washington Commanders – New head coach and rookie QB reset the franchise's direction.

NFC North

  • Chicago Bears – Caleb Williams, the #1 pick, leads a new era with DJ Moore and Rome Odunze.
  • Detroit Lions – Jared Goff & Dan Campbell look to build on 2024 playoff success.
  • Green Bay Packers – Jordan Love leads a rising young team with postseason hopes.
  • Minnesota Vikings – Rookie QB J.J. McCarthy takes the reins after Kirk Cousins’ departure.

NFC South

  • Atlanta Falcons – Kirk Cousins and Bijan Robinson aim to dominate a weak division.
  • Carolina Panthers – Bryce Young seeks a Year 2 leap under a rebuilt coaching staff.
  • New Orleans Saints – Veteran core led by Derek Carr keeps them competitive.
  • Tampa Bay Buccaneers – Baker Mayfield returns after surprising 2024 campaign success.

NFC West

  • Arizona Cardinals – Kyler Murray returns with something to prove and a healthy roster.
  • Los Angeles Rams – Sean McVay’s squad still features stars like Cooper Kupp and Aaron Donald.
  • San Francisco 49ers – Brock Purdy, Christian McCaffrey, and the league’s best defense remain elite.
  • Seattle Seahawks – Geno Smith, young defense, and dynamic WRs keep Seattle in the mix.
